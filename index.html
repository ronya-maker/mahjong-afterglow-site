Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to roskarpach@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few days.

Privacy Policy

Mahjong Afterglow (“the game”) is developed by Rostyslav Karpach. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.

What the game stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings, nickname and avatar) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers, we do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or photos. A photo you choose as an avatar never leaves your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location based on IP address, and information about ad interactions and app usage. On iPhone you decide whether apps may track you (App Tracking Transparency); the game works the same either way. Users in the EU/EEA and UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying “No ads” removes ads between levels.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in your iPhone settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking) and delete all game data by deleting the app. For any privacy question, contact roskarpach@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
